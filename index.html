Interwar & WWII Interactive Timeline

Explore key historical milestones chronologically from 1919 Peace Treaties through to 1945.

1919
June 1919

Signing of the Treaty of Versailles

Germany is forced to sign the Treaty of Versailles, taking full blame for WWI (Clause 231 - War Guilt Clause), facing severe territorial losses, military restrictions, and massive financial reparations (£6.6 billion).

Ch 1: Peace Settlements
1920
Jan 1920

Establishment of the League of Nations

Founded to promote international cooperation and maintain world peace. However, major powers like the USA never join, and USSR/Germany are initially excluded.

Ch 2: League of Nations
1922
Oct 1922

Mussolini's March on Rome

Benito Mussolini and his Fascist Blackshirts stage a march on Rome. King Victor Emmanuel III appoints Mussolini Prime Minister, inaugurating Fascist dictatorship in Italy.

Ch 3: Rise of Dictators
1929
Oct 1929

Wall Street Crash & Great Depression

Stock market crash triggers global economic collapse. Extreme poverty and unemployment fuel political radicalism in Germany and militarism in Japan.

Ch 3 & 5: Global Crisis
1931
Sept 1931

Mukden Incident & Invasion of Manchuria

Japanese Kwantung Army stages a bomb attack near Mukden, using it as a pretext to invade Manchuria. The League of Nations fails to act decisively, leading Japan to leave the League.

Ch 2 & 5: WWII in Asia
1933
Jan 1933

Hitler Appointed Chancellor of Germany

Exploiting fear of Communism and economic distress, Adolf Hitler is appointed Chancellor and quickly establishes total totalitarian rule, dismantling the Weimar Republic.

Ch 3: Rise of Dictators
1935
Oct 1935

Abyssinian Crisis

Mussolini invades Abyssinia (Ethiopia). The League imposes half-hearted sanctions excluding oil and failing to close the Suez Canal, completely destroying the League's credibility.

Ch 2: League of Nations
1938
Sept 1938

Munich Agreement & Policy of Appeasement

Britain and France yield to Hitler's demand for the Sudetenland in Czechoslovakia. Neville Chamberlain declares "Peace for our time", but Hitler breaks the pact months later.

Ch 4: WWII Europe
1939
Sept 1, 1939

Invasion of Poland & Outbreak of WWII in Europe

Germany invades Poland after signing the Nazi-Soviet Non-Aggression Pact. Britain and France declare war on Germany two days later, starting WWII in Europe.

Ch 4: WWII Europe
1941
Dec 7, 1941

Pearl Harbor Attack & War in the Pacific

Japan launches a surprise attack on the US naval base at Pearl Harbor, Hawaii. The USA enters World War II on the Allied side.
